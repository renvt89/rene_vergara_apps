Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-02 · Online: https://renvt89.github.io/rene_vergara_apps/onepercent/ Developer / data controller: Rene Vergara — renvt89@gmail.com — Avenida Paseo Amalfi 3600, Int. 137, Zapopan, Jalisco, Mexico, C.P. 45066 App: OnePercent (Android, package com.renvt89.onepercent)

OnePercent is a Kaizen self-improvement app. It works fully offline. This policy explains what happens to your information. The same text is shown inside the app under Me → Settings → Privacy policy.

1. Your content stays on your device

Everything you enter — life areas, goals, 1% Steps, completions, streaks, 5S spaces and their optional before/after photos, weekly reviews, ratings, 5 Whys analyses, Life Wheel scores, XP and badges — is stored only on your device in a local database and local settings file. OnePercent has no user accounts and no server. We (the developer) never collect, receive, read, upload, share or sell your content.

2. No advertising

OnePercent shows no ads. It contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not use your device's advertising ID.

3. Optional tips — Google Play Billing

You can optionally support OnePercent with a one-off tip ("Support OnePercent"). Tips are processed entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether a tip (or the earlier "Remove Ads" purchase from version 1.0) went through, so it can say thanks and stop asking. Every feature is free either way. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13+ asks you): used only for your daily reminder, weekly review reminder and achievement notifications. Optional.
  • Internet / billing: added by Google Play Billing; used only for the optional tips.
  • Network state / wake lock / boot completed: added by Android's WorkManager to run your reminders.
  • No storage, location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions. Photos for 5S spaces are chosen with the system photo picker, which shares only the photo you pick; a resized copy is kept in the app's private storage.

5. Backups

  • Export/Import: you can export all app data to a JSON file you choose (and import it later) using Android's file picker. The file goes only where you save it. Photos are not included.
  • Android backup: if you have enabled Android's device backup, Android may include this app's data in your personal backup in your Google account. You control this in system settings.

6. Children

OnePercent is not directed at children. It is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because your data never leaves your device (other than the billing data Google Play processes for an optional tip), you delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. There is no server copy to request deletion of.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data — for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the California CCPA/CPRA, or Mexico's ARCO rights (Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares). Because OnePercent collects no personal data, we hold nothing to hand over, correct or erase: you can see, edit, export or delete all your data yourself inside the app. We do not sell or share personal information. For any question or request, write to renvt89@gmail.com.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and in the app, with a new "Last updated" date.
