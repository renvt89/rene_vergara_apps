Summary

The app works on your device. Your entries stay on your device unless you use one of the optional features described below.

Data stored on your device

What you enter is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it. Device backups made by your phone's operating system (for example Google backup) are governed by your device provider's terms.

Optional cloud sync

Cloud sync is off until you turn it on. When it is on, the app sends the data it saves (your entries in this app) to a server operated by the developer, together with a random device identifier and pairing token created by the app, your device platform (Android or web), and sync timestamps. The server also receives your IP address as part of the connection. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.

Purpose and legal basis: to back up your data and keep your devices in sync, based on your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a); Mexico LFPDPPP), which you can withdraw at any time by turning sync off.

Retention and deletion: synced data is kept until you delete it with "Delete my cloud data" in the app or ask us by email; turning sync off stops new uploads. Technical server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and operation.

Location of processing: the server may be hosted outside your country. Where data leaves the EEA/UK we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) and data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Transfers: we do not transfer synced data to third parties. A hosting provider may store it on our behalf, only under our instructions.

Photos and files you choose

Files or photos you pick are processed only on your device to do what you ask (for example resize, decode or show them). They are not uploaded.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. If you allow notifications, no notification server is involved and nothing is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Purchases through Google Play

Payments are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We never receive your card or bank details. Google shares with us order information needed to deliver and account for your purchase — order number, product, price, date and country or region. We use it to provide what you bought, handle refunds and meet tax and accounting obligations (legal bases: contract and legal obligation), and keep it for as long as tax law requires (in Mexico, generally 5 years).

Commitment refund requests

If you request a refund for a completed commitment, the app opens your email app with a pre-filled message. When you send it, we receive your email address and the details in the message (order number, habit name, dates and amount). We use them only to verify and process your refund and to reply to you, and keep them as long as needed for that and for tax records.

If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites, no profiling or automated decisions about you. We do not sell or share personal information (as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act). The app uses your device's local storage only to work; it does not use tracking cookies, so no cookie consent is needed.
